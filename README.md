# RBM Traders - Readymade Wall Solutions

A professional website for RBM Traders, specializing in readymade wall construction and supply services.

## Features

- **Bilingual Support**: English and Hindi language options
- **Interactive Wall Calculator**: Calculate costs for cemented boundary walls
- **Project Portfolio**: Showcase of completed wall construction projects
- **Responsive Design**: Mobile-friendly interface
- **WhatsApp Integration**: Direct contact through WhatsApp
- **Professional Gallery**: Lightbox image viewing for project photos

## Pages

### Main Page (index.html)
- Company introduction and services
- Product specifications and pricing
- Contact information
- WhatsApp integration for quick inquiries

### Quotation Pages
- **Instant Quotation** (`quotation/instant-quotation.html`): Interactive calculator for wall cost estimation
- **Wall Calculator** (`quotation/wall-calculator.html`): Simplified calculator interface

### Work Portfolio (`work-done/index.html`)
- Gallery of completed projects
- Project statistics
- Contact call-to-action

## Services Offered

- **Cemented Boundary Walls**: Pre-cast concrete walls for property boundaries
- **Professional Installation**: Complete installation service from foundation to finish
- **Timely Delivery**: Reliable supply chain with 24-48 hour delivery to nearby villages
- **Quality Assurance**: 5-year structural warranty
